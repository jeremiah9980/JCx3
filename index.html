The 3x Playbook

Start with about $13,500 from the medical claims. Finish 24 months later with $40,000 working for the family. No lottery tickets doing the heavy lifting: three engines run at once, and a fourth stays small on purpose.

The four engines

KEEP
Reserve + Roth · $6,500 day one

High-yield savings at ~4% and a Roth IRA in index funds. Steady, boring, and never touched for the bet. This is the floor the other engines stand on.

EARN
Brace business · $1,500 day one

Jesica's LLC selling knee and elbow braces. Capital turns four times a year at roughly 2x markup. The only engine that can multiply inside twelve months, because hours are the fuel.

ADD
Cash flow · $500 per month + bonus

A fixed transfer every payday plus the Q1 bonus each year. Does more of the tripling than any rate of return possibly can at this horizon.

BET
Venture slice · $750 day one, 10% cap

Reg CF startups or a small-cap ETF. Written off the day it's placed. If it hits, two-thirds moves to KEEP.

Payday rhythm

Every other Friday, three transfers, ten minutes, no decisions:

1 · ADD$250 to high-yield savings (becomes Roth once the reserve is full)

3 · LOGMileage log reconciled, receipts photographed into the business folder

Rules that keep it 3x instead of 0.5x

  • The bet never exceeds 10% of everything in the plan. If it grows past that, sell down to 10% and move the rest to KEEP.
  • The reserve is never inventory money and never bet money. The business grows only from its own profit and its scheduled slices.
  • No third SKU until the first two have sold through twice.
  • The truck stays a personal loan at the excellent-credit rate; the business reimburses miles or takes the depreciation on paper. Never a commercial loan on a months-old LLC.
  • If the LLC has no sales by June 2027, the business capital rolls to the Roth and the plan still reaches 3x by month 30 on ADD alone. Nothing is lost but time.
  • Any windfall not on this page (refund, side job, gift) follows the bonus split. No exceptions, no re-planning.
